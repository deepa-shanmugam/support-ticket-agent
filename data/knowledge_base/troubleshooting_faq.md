# Troubleshooting FAQ

## App Crashes / Won't Open

1. **Force-close the app** and reopen it.
2. **Check for updates** — ensure you have the latest version from your app store.
3. **Restart your device** to clear any temporary issues.
4. **Reinstall the app** if the issue persists (your data is stored in the cloud and will sync after login).
5. If still crashing, **report the issue** to support with your device model, OS version, and app version number.

## Slow Performance

- Ensure you have a stable internet connection (Wi-Fi preferred over mobile data for large operations).
- Close other apps running in the background.
- Clear the app cache from device settings.
- If performance issues persist, contact support with details about the affected feature.

## Data Not Syncing

- Check your internet connection.
- Log out and log back in to force a sync.
- Ensure the app has permission to run in the background in your device settings.
- Check the service status page for any ongoing outages.

## Feature Not Working as Expected

- Verify the feature is available on your current plan (some features are plan-specific).
- Check the in-app Help Center for feature-specific guides.
- If the feature was recently changed, check the release notes for updates.

## Export / Download Issues

- Data exports are available from **Account Settings → Data → Export**.
- Large exports may take up to 30 minutes. You will receive an email with a download link.
- Exports are retained for 48 hours — download promptly.

## Escalation

For persistent technical issues not resolved by the above steps, escalate to the technical support team with device logs if available.
