# Subscription Policy

## Plan Types

- **Monthly Plan**: Billed every 30 days. Can be cancelled at any time.
- **Annual Plan**: Billed once per year. Discounted rate vs. monthly.

## Cancellation

- Cancellations take effect at the **end of the current billing period**.
- No partial refunds are issued for unused days within an active billing cycle, except under the refund eligibility window (see Refund Policy).
- To cancel, go to **Account Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription**.

## Upgrades and Downgrades

- Upgrading from monthly to annual takes effect **immediately**. The remaining days on the monthly plan are prorated toward the annual plan cost.
- Downgrading from annual to monthly takes effect at the **end of the annual billing period**.

## Pausing a Subscription

Subscription pausing is not currently available. Customers wishing to pause service should cancel and re-subscribe when ready. Previous data is retained for 90 days after cancellation.

## Auto-Renewal

All plans auto-renew by default. Customers receive an email reminder 7 days before each renewal. Auto-renewal can be disabled in **Account Settings → Billing**.

## Escalation

Billing disputes beyond standard policy (e.g., double charges, unauthorized renewals) must be escalated to the billing team.
