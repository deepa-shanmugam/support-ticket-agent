# Account Access FAQ

## Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

Visit the login page and click **"Forgot Password"**. Enter your registered email address and you will receive a reset link within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

## Q: My account is locked. What do I do?

Accounts are locked after 5 consecutive failed login attempts. Wait 15 minutes and try again, or use the password reset flow to unlock immediately.

## Q: I'm not receiving two-factor authentication codes.

- Ensure your phone number or authenticator app is correctly configured in **Account Settings → Security**.
- For SMS codes, check that your phone has signal and is not blocking messages from short codes.
- If using an authenticator app, ensure the device time is synchronized.
- If the issue persists, contact support to temporarily disable 2FA and re-enroll.

## Q: I can't log in even with the correct password.

- Clear your browser cache and cookies, then try again.
- Try an incognito/private browsing window.
- Disable browser extensions that may interfere with login forms.
- If using SSO (single sign-on), contact your IT administrator.

## Q: My account was accessed without my permission.

Immediately reset your password and revoke all active sessions from **Account Settings → Active Sessions**. Then contact support to report the incident and have the security team investigate.

## Escalation

Account access issues involving suspected unauthorized access or identity verification must be escalated to a human support specialist.
