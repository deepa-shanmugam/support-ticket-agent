# Refund Policy

## Eligibility

Customers are eligible for a full refund under the following conditions:

1. The refund request is submitted **within 7 days of billing**.
2. The product or service was **not actively used** after the renewal date.

## Procedure

- Submit a refund request via the support portal or contact support directly.
- Provide your account email and the billing date in question.
- Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method.

## Escalation Rules

- Refund requests **outside the 7-day window** must be escalated to a human support specialist for review.
- **Repeated refund requests** from the same account within a 90-day period must be escalated for review before any approval.
- Cases involving suspected abuse of the refund policy must be flagged and escalated.

## Important Note for Agents

> If no applicable refund policy clause is found for a customer's situation, the agent must state that the policy could not be verified and escalate the ticket. The agent must never fabricate a policy response.
